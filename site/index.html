MASTERED
CRAFTSMANSHIP.

At NAVARIS BOATS, we are dedicated to building high-grade boats ranging from 4.8m to 10.5m. Engineered with Marine Grade 5083 5mm aluminum, heavy-duty Achilles Hypalon, and reinforced deep-V hulls, every vessel is the result of three generations of hands-on sea testing and continuous refinement. Driven by unbeatable value and uncompromising build quality, our mission is to elevate your on-water capability. Proudly designed and based in the port of Ploče, Croatia.

ADRIATIC TESTED
MLJET & LASTOVO.

The open waters surrounding Mljet and Lastovo are world-famous for their beauty, but infamous for punishing afternoon swells and relentless chop. That is our backyard and our testing laboratory.

Running our own rental fleet gives us a rare engineering edge: we see firsthand how hulls, tubes, and hardware endure under real commercial workloads. Every NAVARIS model undergoes extensive offshore sea trials to eliminate weak points, perfect hull geometry, and guarantee a bone-dry, fuel-efficient ride.
